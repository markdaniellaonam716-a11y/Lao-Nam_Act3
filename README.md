<h1>Daniel's Crispy Chicken Tenders Recipe</h1>

<h2>Ingredients</h2>


<ul>
  <li>4 chicken breasts , boneless, skinless</li>
  <li>1 cup buttermilk</li>
  <li>1 teaspoon hot sauce</li>
  <li>2 large eggs , beaten</li>
  <li>2 cups flour</li>
  <li>2 1/2 teaspoons kosher salt</li>
  <li>1/8 teaspoon baking powder</li>
  <li>vegetable oil , for frying</li>
  <li>3/4 teaspoon course ground black pepper</li>
  <li>1/8 teaspoon paprika</li>
  <li>1/8 teaspoon garlic powder</li>
</ul>

<h2>Instructions</h2>
<ol>
  <li>Cut the chicken breasts into four tenders each and soak them in a bowl with the buttermilk and hot sauce for 30 minutes.</li>
  <li>Transfer the chicken to a plate and let them drain.</li>
  <li>In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour, salt, baking powder, pepper, paprika, and garlic powder.</li>
  <li>Dredge the chicken tenders in the flour mixture, shaking off any excess.</li>
  <li>Heat the vegetable oil in a deep fryer or large skillet to 375°F (190°C).</li>
  <li>Fry the chicken tenders for 3-4 minutes on each side, or until golden brown and cooked through.</li>
<li>Remove the chicken tenders from the oil and place them on a paper towel-lined plate to drain any excess oil.</li>
</ol>
